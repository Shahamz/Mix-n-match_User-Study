Research study · about 10 minutes

Which picture works better?

We are testing ways of getting a computer to draw a single picture from several separate written descriptions — one for the sky, another for what sits underneath it, and so on.

Different methods join those pieces together in different ways, and the only real test is whether the result looks right to a person. So we would like your eye. You will see pictures side by side and choose the one that works better. There are no right answers and nothing is being tested about you.

How to answer

Go with your first impression

Each screen asks one short question, shows a description, and gives you two pictures — occasionally four. Pick the one that answers the question better. A few seconds per screen is exactly right; deliberating rarely helps.

Whole or part
Some questions show a complete picture. Others show a single part of one, and a small diagram beside the description marks which part.
Shapes differ
Pictures in a pair are often different shapes or sizes. That comes from how each method works, not from a mistake. Judge what is in them.
Four pictures
Now and then you will pick the best of four, then the worst of the rest.
Keyboard
A and B, or the arrow keys. Number keys 1 to 4 when there are four.
